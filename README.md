## ◈ About

I am a computer science undergraduate with a keen interest in the
intellectual frontiers of computing. Alongside developing web
applications, I am cultivating a deeper understanding of **quantum
computing**, its mathematical foundations, and its prospective
computational applications.

My interests lie at the intersection of **software engineering,
algorithms, and emerging computational paradigms**. I value deliberate
learning, rigorous problem-solving, and the craft of building technology
that is both useful and thoughtfully engineered.

- 🔭 **Currently exploring:** Frontend engineering, computer science
  fundamentals, and quantum computing.
- ⚛️ **Long-term ambition:** To pursue research in quantum computing and
  contribute to the field's evolving body of knowledge.
- 🧠 **Learning philosophy:** Strong fundamentals first; experimentation
  and implementation alongside theory.
